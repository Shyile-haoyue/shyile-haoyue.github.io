Current Research

Master’s Project

My master’s project evaluates the effects of China’s National Modern Agricultural Industrial Park program on agricultural development.

It combines policy document analysis with causal inference, paying particular attention to changes in policy priorities, lagged effects, and differences across implementation cohorts.

Methods

My methodological toolkit includes difference-in-differences, randomized controlled trials, instrumental variables, and list experiments.

I am particularly interested in combining different methods to better understand both the effects of public policy and how those effects emerge in practice.

Software & Fieldwork

I use R, Python, and NVivo for statistical analysis, qualitative coding, data management, and data visualization.

I also have practical experience in field research and primary-data collection.

Working papers, my master’s thesis, and detailed research proposals are not posted publicly. Selected materials are available upon request.
